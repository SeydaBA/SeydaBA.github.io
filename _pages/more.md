Beyond research, some of the things I care about and enjoy:

- **Music:** Music has been part of my life since high school. I began with the _ney_, the traditional Turkish reed flute, and stayed with it for seven years — long enough for it to shape how I listen to everything else. I later spent two years studying the _tanbur_ with supervision of [Özer Özel](https://www.youtube.com/OzerOzel), alongside lessons in _makam_, the modal system at the heart of Turkish classical music. I have also trained in traditional percussion (_bendir_ and _kudüm_).
- **Sports & Mind Sports:** Tennis and chess.
- **Volunteering:** Volunteered with TİKA (Turkish Cooperation and Coordination Agency) in North Macedonia, assisting local students, distributing school supplies, and supporting school improvement activities.


Outside all of this, I love to travel — discovering new places and meeting new people is the part I enjoy most. Lately I have taken up photography as well, which has become a good excuse to look at familiar places a little more slowly.
